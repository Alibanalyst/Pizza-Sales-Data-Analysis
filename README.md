# Pizza Sales Data Analysis, Dashboard & MIS Reporting

## Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data to understand overall sales performance, product performance, category performance, pizza-size contribution, and operational sales patterns.

The project was developed using Microsoft Excel and Power Query, with PivotTables, the Excel Data Model, calculated metrics, an interactive dashboard, and a structured MIS report.

The workflow covers the complete process from raw data preparation to business analysis, visualization, and management reporting.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze pizza sales data and identify patterns in:

- Revenue
- Orders
- Pizza sales volume
- Product performance
- Category performance
- Pizza-size contribution
- Monthly sales trends
- Day and time-based sales patterns

The analysis is designed to support sales monitoring and identify areas for further business investigation.

## Dataset

The project uses four related datasets:

- Orders
- Order Details
- Pizzas
- Pizza Types

After combining and transforming the datasets, the final analytical dataset contains 48,620 records.

## Data Preparation

Power Query was used to prepare the data for analysis.

The main data preparation steps included:

- Combining multiple related tables
- Merging Orders, Order Details, Pizzas, and Pizza Types
- Setting appropriate data types
- Calculating Revenue
- Creating Year and Month fields
- Creating Day and Day Number fields
- Creating Order Hour
- Creating Time Period
- Cleaning text fields
- Validating the final dataset

## Analytical Approach

The analysis was structured around the following areas:

1. Overall Sales Performance
2. Monthly Sales Performance
3. Product Performance
4. Category Performance
5. Pizza Size Performance
6. Operational Performance

PivotTables and calculated metrics were used to analyze revenue, order volume, pizza volume, and other business KPIs.

## Key Findings

- Total revenue was $817,860.05 from 21,350 orders.
- A total of 49,574 pizzas were sold.
- July recorded the highest monthly revenue at $72,557.90.
- October recorded the lowest monthly revenue at $64,027.60.
- The Thai Chicken Pizza generated the highest revenue among individual pizzas.
- Classic was the leading category by revenue and pizza volume.
- Large pizzas generated the highest revenue and sales volume among pizza sizes.
- Afternoon was the highest-revenue time period.
- Friday recorded the highest day-level revenue.
- 12:00 PM was the highest individual revenue hour.

These findings describe observed sales patterns and provide a basis for further investigation.
